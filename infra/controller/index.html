Ditto Relay synchronization

Live receives new events. Live catch-up repairs missed intervals, beginning with the last hour on a fresh install. Backfill adds an older hour only when requested. Authenticated private sessions fetch each participating user's accessible history separately.

Live uses its own peer connection. Live catch-up and backfill share another connection, but their jobs and event counts are separate. Accepted transfers may include duplicates. Combined traffic includes private sync and protocol messages. Counters reset when the worker restarts.
